HOW IT WORKS

From Assessment to Opportunity — in One Platform

Traditional LMS: Course → Learn → Quiz → Certificate.
Capacity Connect: Assess → Identify Gap → Personalize → Learn → Prove → Verify → Opportunity.

1

Skill Assessment

Take a short targeted assessment. We measure where you actually are, not where you think you are.

2

Gap Analysis

See exactly which skills you're missing for your target role. Prioritized by gap size, colored by severity.

3

Personalized Path

A learning path built from your gaps — not a generic curriculum. Strong skills are skipped entirely.

4

Practice & Build

Learn through modules and a real project. Apply every skill you've studied in a full-stack context.

5

Prove & Connect

Practical assessment proves your skill. Get a Skill Passport. Match to real opportunities by verified skills.

THE CORE INNOVATION

Not Just Courses. Verified Competency.

Traditional LMS tells you what courses to complete. Capacity Connect tells you which skills you are missing, creates a path to close those gaps, checks whether you actually gained the skill, and then connects your verified skills to opportunities.
